  • The property was approved on January 20, 2026 to be annexed for residential development.

This on-the-record statement indicates that the effluent discharge line — the pipe carrying treated water from the existing plant to the Cumberland River — already runs through the parcel immediately adjacent to Site 22. Mayor’s Remarks on Wilson County Commission Resolution

Mayor Rick Bell addressed the Wilson County Commission’s resolution, passed the previous evening, opposing certain potential sewer plant locations. He reviewed the history of city sewer service provided to county facilities, including schools, the county jail, the Expo Center, and commercial development along I-840. County facilities on the city’s sewer system referenced by the mayor included:

  • Lebanon High School, Wilson Central High School, Southside, Adult Learning Center, Adult High School, and the Barry Tatum Academy (all within city limits)
  • Glavo Elementary and Glavo Middle (outside city limits, on city sewer)
  • Laredo Elementary and Central Pike Elementary (recently approved connections)
  • Wilson County Jail complex
  • County office buildings throughout the city
  • I-840 corridor commercial development (warehouses, gas stations)
  • The Expo Center, which the city funds $100,000 per year for 20 years

Mayor Bell concluded his remarks (19:01–19:17):

“I’m saying tonight this sewer plant will be built. The committee is going to pick a site and we’re going to buy it. And some county commission resolution is not going to stop us.”

Councilor Response

Following the mayor’s remarks, one councilor (29:45–30:11) offered this statement:

I’m hoping out of all of those sites that we will have some willing sellers and we will be able to negotiate a deal and we will put a brand new wastewater system in that will service all of Lebanon and obviously some of these other places that are not in the city limits.”
