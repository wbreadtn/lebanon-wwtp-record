Mayor’s Comments

He also acknowledged the death of former City Councilor Fred Burton, who served on the council for over 30 years, and read into the record the Legends of Lebanon Award previously presented to Burton. (Fred Burton’s celebration of life was scheduled for Saturday, July 11, at 4:00 p.m. at the Wilson County Expo Center, with a lying-in-state at City Hall from 10:00 a.m. to noon.)

Legislative Action — City-County Relations (Context)

Ordinance 267445 (first reading) — Termination of the city’s annual financial contribution to the Wilson County Expo Center ($100,000/year). Deferred to the next regular meeting at Councilor Brian’s request, to allow all council members to be present. Discussion confirmed that the original ordinance was written as a discretionary donation (“up to $100,000 per year, up to 20 years”) and does not represent a binding legal obligation. Councilors Brian and Morehead both framed the potential termination as a taxpayer-interest decision rather than retaliation against the county. The Expo Center has grown substantially since the donation began and now hosts the Tennessee State Fair.

Note: While this ordinance is not directly part of the WWTP site selection process, it arises in the same context of city-county tensions that intensified after the Wilson County Commission passed a resolution opposing potential sewer plant locations in the county. It is included here as relevant background.
